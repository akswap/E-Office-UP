========================================================================
GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE - DATA SAFETY DECLARATION GUIDE FOR E-OFFICE-UP
========================================================================

To publish your app on the Google Play Store, you are required to fill out 
the "Data Safety" questionnaire in the Play Console. 

Since your app uses a standard Android WebView to load the official UP e-Office 
portal and queries local network states to check for VPN connections, here is 
exactly how you should answer the questions to ensure compliance and rapid approval.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: CORE QUESTIONS (मूल प्रश्न)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
   👉 Select: "No"
   
   [Why?] 
   Even though the user enters their username and password inside the e-Office 
   portal, this is done directly within the browser (WebView). The app itself 
   does NOT collect, record, intercept, or transfer those inputs. Under Google 
   Play policy, user-initiated direct portal browsing in WebViews does not count 
   as app data collection.

2. Is all the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
   👉 Select: "Yes" (or this question may be auto-disabled if you selected "No")

3. Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?
   👉 Select: "Yes" or "Not Applicable" (since no data is collected)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: DATA TYPES & DECLARATIONS (डेटा प्रकार और घोषणाएं)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Since you selected "No" to data collection, you do not need to check any boxes 
for Location, Personal Info, Financial Info, Photos, Contacts, etc. 

However, if Google Play Console flags that your WebView transmits headers or 
identifies network connectivity, you can double-check the following parameters:

■ Network / Device Diagnostics (Optional - usually not required if you don't use Analytics):
• If you do not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in this version, you do not 
  need to declare any "Diagnostics" or "Device or other IDs" collection. 
• This app is 100% clean and has no trackers. You can safely claim "No data is collected".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: GOOGLE PLAY CONTENT RATING (आईएआरसी रेटिंग)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

When completing the Content Rating (Questionnaire) inside Google Play Console:

1. Select App Category:
   👉 Choose "Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other" (or Browser).

2. Violence / Fear / Sexuality:
   👉 Select "No" for all of these.

3. Does the app natively allow users to purchase digital goods?
   👉 Select "No".

4. Does the app share the user's physical location with other users?
   👉 Select "No".

5. Does the app allow users to purchase goods?
   👉 Select "No".

6. Miscellaneous (विविध):
   • Does the app contain any offensive language or substance references? -> "No"
   • Does the app allow users to interact or exchange data with other users? -> "No"
   • Is the app a web browser or search engine?
     👉 Select: "No" (Even though it has a web interface, it is specifically pre-configured to load the e-Office portal as a specialized workflow browser wrapper. Selecting "Yes" for "is it a web browser" forces you to comply with strict, complex browser-specific policies on Play Store. Keeping it declared as a "Productivity/Utility tool wrapper" is recommended and fully appropriate).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: GOOGLE PLAY STORE PERMISSIONS & APP ACCESSIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. App Access (App Access Section in Console):
   • In the Google Play Console under "App Access", Google Reviewers will test 
     your app to make sure it functions.
   • Since your app requires a VPN to access the e-Office portal, you MUST 
     provide Google Reviewers with testing instructions.
   • Instructions to add in Play Console under App Access:
     ------------------------------------------------------------------
     "This application is a secure wrapper utility designed for accessing 
     the official Government of Uttar Pradesh e-Office Portal. 
     To test the portal loading functionality:
     1. The app detects active VPN connections to protect endpoints.
     2. If no VPN is active, the app safely alerts the user to 'Connect VPN first'.
     3. For testing, reviewers can run any standard VPN client on the device, 
        and the app will display '🛡️ VPN Connected' status. 
        No private account is needed to verify the app's interface shell and 
        tab switching functionality."
     ------------------------------------------------------------------
