# Privacy Policy for E-Office-UP Secure Browser

**Last Updated:** June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **E-Office-UP Secure Browser** mobile application ("the App") handles information and data on your Android device. 

The App is designed as a secure, lightweight browser wrapper for accessing the official e-Office portal of Uttar Pradesh (https://districts.upeoffice.gov.in). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, primarily government employees, administrators, and personnel.

---

### 1. Independent Status Disclaimer
The App is an independent, utility-based browser wrapper developed to facilitate convenient and secure access to public government web portals for authorized personnel. 
* **The App is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or an official product of any government department, agency, or authority.**
* **The App does not represent the Government of India or the Government of Uttar Pradesh.**

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use
We believe in absolute data privacy and transparency. **The App does not collect, log, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user session credentials.**

* **No Credentials Tracking:** Your e-Office usernames, passwords, OTPs, or active session cookies are loaded directly inside the standard Android system WebView component. These credentials are transmitted directly and securely to the official e-Office servers. We have absolutely no visibility or access to your credentials or active sessions.
* **No Database Logging:** The App does not contain any remote databases, analytics libraries, or third-party tracking software.
* **Network Status Check:** To check if a secure government VPN connection is active before you log in, the App queries local system network properties. This status check is performed entirely offline, locally on your device, and is never logged or sent to any remote server.

---

### 3. Session and Cookie Isolation (Private Mode)
To ensure data security when multiple employees share a device:
* The App provides **Normal** and **Private** tabs.
* Private tabs utilize Android's multi-profile WebView framework, which segregates the local cookie jars and browsing history completely from normal tabs.
* Closing a Private Tab ensures that those specific session identifiers are cleared, preventing unauthorized auto-logins.

---

### 4. Permissions Requested and How They Are Used
The App requires the following standard Android permissions to operate:
* **INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to load the web-based e-Office portal pages.
* **ACCESS NETWORK STATE (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Required to check if your mobile device is connected to the internet and to identify active VPN network interfaces.

We do NOT request sensitive device permissions such as Location, Contacts, Camera, Storage, or Microphone.

---

### 5. Third-Party Websites
Since the App loads the official UP e-Office portal, any interactions you have on those portals are governed by their respective official privacy policies. We encourage users to read the privacy policies of the government portals they access.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for professional administrative use by adults (government personnel) and does not target or collect information from children under the age of 13.
