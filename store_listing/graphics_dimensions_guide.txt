========================================================================
GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE - GRAPHICS DIMENSIONS & DESIGN BLUEPRINT
========================================================================

The Google Play Console requires specific graphical assets to publish your app 
and make it look professional. Below are the exact requirements and design 
ideas for each asset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. APP ICON (ऐप आइकॉन)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Dimensions: 512 x 512 pixels
• Format: 32-bit PNG (with alpha / transparency support)
• Max File Size: 1 MB
• Shape: Google Play automatically applies a squircle mask (rounded square) 
  to all icons. Keep your key designs within the safe center zone (approx. 80% 
  of the canvas).

■ Design Blueprint:
• Background: A solid white or light-gray circle floating on a pristine dark-blue 
  or tricolor gradient background.
• Foreground: A highly recognizable vector shape combining a clean paper/folder 
  icon (representing documents) with a bright teal shield and lock icon 
  (representing VPN and session security).
• Saffron/White/Green Accent: A subtle tricolor ribbon or accent on the side 
  of the folder to associate it directly with Uttar Pradesh (India) state portals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FEATURE GRAPHIC (फीचर ग्राफिक)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Dimensions: 1024 x 500 pixels
• Format: PNG or 24-bit JPEG (no alpha/transparency allowed)
• Max File Size: 1 MB
• Purpose: This is the large promotional banner shown at the top of your 
  store page on mobile devices and Google Play search results.

■ Design Blueprint:
• Background: A clean, modern slate-colored or dark navy-blue background 
  with glowing digital elements.
• Left Side: The app title "E-Office-UP" in a bold, elegant sans-serif font, 
  and a short sub-headline: "Secure Gateway for Government Employees".
• Right Side: A premium smartphone mockup showing the app interface with the 
  green "🛡️ VPN Connected" badge clearly visible.
• Trust Badges: Tiny icons depicting "Private Tab Isolation" and "Instant VPN Check" 
  to establish security credibility instantly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PHONE SCREENSHOTS (फ़ोन स्क्रीनशॉट)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Quantity: Minimum 2 screenshots, Maximum 8 screenshots.
• Aspect Ratio: 16:9 or 9:16 (Recommended: vertical 9:16 portrait style, 
  e.g., 1080 x 1920 or 1440 x 2560 pixels).
• Format: PNG or JPEG
• Max File Size: 8 MB per screenshot

■ Screenshot Layout Strategy:
• Screenshot 1: "Secure Landing Dashboard"
  - Image: Show the app home page highlighting the search bar and the 
    pre-configured URL.
  - Text Banner at Top: "Instant Access to UP e-Office districts portal"
• Screenshot 2: "Smart VPN Detection Status"
  - Image: Show the active warning status changing to "🛡️ VPN Connected" 
    when the VPN is enabled on the device.
  - Text Banner at Top: "Automatic safety check ensures secure connections"
• Screenshot 3: "Private Mode Session Isolation"
  - Image: Show the Tab Selector view with two tabs open (Normal tab with 
    active session, and a separate Private tab representing a fresh session).
  - Text Banner at Top: "Dual-session support with complete cookie isolation"

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO CREATE AND EXPORT THESE IMAGES EASILY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Canva / Figma (Recommended): You can use Canva (free) or Figma to create 
  a custom canvas with the exact dimensions (e.g., 1024x500 for Feature Graphic).
• High-Contrast Color Palette: Use a professional color scheme to match 
  modern Android Material 3 themes:
  - Deep Blue: #1E293B (Backgrounds)
  - Saffron Accent: #FF9933
  - Green Accent: #138808
  - Accent / Primary: #1E40AF (Sleek Royal Blue)
